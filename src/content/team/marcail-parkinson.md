Kia Ora! My name is Marcail and I’ve been working and volunteering with Take 10 for a few years now.

I love the work we get to do giving back to the community. It’s always a fun night out with the truck and we get to meet so many awesome people out on Courtenay place.

My favourite thing about being at Take 10 is getting to help people and all the lovely chats that we have. It’s great to provide a safe space for people to come hang out and get support when they need it.

When I’m not at Take10 I love hanging out with my flat whānau and my cat, going on walks around the south coast, baking, and of course a good party!
