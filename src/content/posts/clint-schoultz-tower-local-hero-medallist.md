We're proud to share that our Chairperson, Clint Schoultz, has been named a Tower Local Hero Medallist for Wellington Te Whanganui-a-Tara, one of 100 Local Heroes recognised across Aotearoa as part of the Kiwibank New Zealander of the Year Awards Ngā Tohu Pou Kōhure o Aotearoa.

Coming from the hospitality industry, Clint believes in making a place better than when you found it, especially when it comes to helping young people.

## Take 10

Take 10 started in Te Whanganui-a-Tara Wellington in 2016 with a simple idea: everyone deserves to feel safe on a night out. Every weekend, trained volunteers offer water, first aid, phone charging, safe transport options and a place to regroup without judgement.

The model has since expanded to Tāmaki Makaurau Auckland, and independent economic modelling estimates Take 10 saves the Wellington community between $1.6 million and $8.1 million each year.

## Do Some Good

After hearing that tamariki were arriving at school with empty lunchboxes, Clint helped establish Do Some Good, which now provides kai to 13 schools and kindergartens across Wellington.

## Thank you

This recognition belongs to every volunteer, partner and supporter who makes this mahi possible. Thank you for being part of it.

A big thank you to Tower, Kiwibank and the New Zealander of the Year Awards for celebrating community heroes across Aotearoa. You can find out more at [nzawards.org.nz](https://nzawards.org.nz).
