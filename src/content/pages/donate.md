  Kia ora whānau,


  Thank you for taking the time to consider supporting us.


  Our organisation works to create change within our local communities across
  Wellington, Porirua, and Hutt Valley.


  Our two flagship programmes; Do Some Good and Take 10, offer support, care,
  and create connections, both building strength and resilience within our
  communities that has a rebounding effect throughout the region.


  Do Some Good is a food support programme providing kai to tamariki who need it
  at local schools and kindergartens across the region, so that all tamariki are
  able to thrive and flourish.


  Take 10 is a programme supporting students and others who are out at night to
  have safe, great nights out in the city, supported by security staff and
  council.


  We have also recently expanded into Tāmaki Makaurau (Auckland), with Take 10
  opening in the city in July 2025, in the Quay Street District.


  We know our work makes a real difference.


  There are a few ways you can support us, namely through volunteering your time
  and skills, sponsoring a school, or else making a donation.


  If you’d like to volunteer your time or sponsor a school please email
  [hello@vsctrust.org.nz](mailto:hello@vsctrust.org.nz) and our Operations
  Manager, Leigh Keown, will get in touch.


  If you choose to donate to us, know that you are choosing to make a difference
  too.
